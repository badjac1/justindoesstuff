For over a decade, web design flattened into uniform, minimalist white boxes. While efficient, it stripped personal homepages of character.

Designing digital spaces that mimic physical objects—wooden shelves, brass labels, parchment cards—gives visitors an immediate sense of place and personal hospitality.
