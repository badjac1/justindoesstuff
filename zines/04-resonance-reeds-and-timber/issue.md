Playing a tenor banjo in traditional sessions is an exercise in acoustic balancing. The skin head tension, bridge timber density, and pick attack shape how the instrument cuts through accordion and fiddle tones.

When shifting between acoustic sessions and home digital recordings, maintaining that warm physical timbre requires mic placement strategy over artificial EQ pushing.
